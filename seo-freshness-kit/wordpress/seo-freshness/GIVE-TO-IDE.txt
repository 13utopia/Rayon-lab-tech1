WordPress-only: this folder is a complete plugin.

1. It must live at wp-content/plugins/seo-freshness/seo-freshness.php
2. Activate plugin, then Settings → Permalinks → Save
3. Settings → SEO Freshness: fill brand/city/phone from THIS site
4. GET /wp-json/seo-freshness/v1/health
5. GET /wp-json/seo-freshness/v1/publish?key=SECRET
6. Open /updates/ in the browser

Do not copy api/*.js into WordPress.
